## 1. Account Registration

To access the platform, you must first receive an invitation from an administrator.

- An admin will send an invite to your email, already associated with your client and project.
- The email will include a registration link where you can set your password.
- The link expires after **15 days**. If it expires, a new invitation must be requested.

After completing registration, you will be able to log in and access assistants related to your assigned project.

### 1.2 Password Recovery

If you forget your password:

- Click the **"Forgot my Password"** link on the login page.
- Follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password.

## 2. Chat Use

- Available assistants appear on the homepage and under the **"Assistants"** tab.
- Use the **"View All"** option to see the full list if multiple assistants are available.

### Starting a Chat

Once you start a new chat, you can:

- Send messages
- Upload files
- Favorite messages
- Copy messages

### Chat History

- After starting a conversation, it is automatically saved.
- You can revisit previous chats through the **"Chat History"** section in the sidebar.
- This section only appears after at least one chat has been created.

---

## 3. Search

- Use the search feature to find messages within your chats.
- Searches include both **user messages** and **assistant responses**.

---

## 4. Favorites

- You can mark important messages as favorites.
- Favorited messages are accessible via the **"Favorites"** section in the sidebar.

---

## 5. Dashboard

- Dashboards can only be created and managed by administrators.
- If your dashboard appears empty, contact an admin for access or configuration.
